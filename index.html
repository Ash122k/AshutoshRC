About

I am a graduate student in mathematics at Boston University. I am co-advised by Keerthi Madapusi at Boston College and Alexander Bertoloni Meli at Boston University.

Before coming to Boston University, I was an undergraduate at Chennai Mathematical Institute and completed my master's at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai .

Research interests

I am interested in arithmetic geometry and algebraic geometry. I am currently working towards a theory of log syntomification for log schemes, following the theory developed by Bhatt, Lurie, and Drinfeld.
